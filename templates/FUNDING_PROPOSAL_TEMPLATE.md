# Funding Guidance (Quick Reference)

Based on the **2026-2027 Funding Guide**.

### 💰 Funding Caps
*   **Per Semester:** $2,000 max per GRSO (Recommended to budget for **$1,000 - $1,500** due to demand).
*   **Per Round (Continuing):** $500 max.
*   **Per Round (Large-Project):** $2,000 max (for events > $500, limited to 2 days).
*   **Food & Beverage:** 
    *   **$20.00** per UCB graduate student attendee.
    *   **$1.00** per other attendee (undergraduates, staff, etc.).
*   **Durable Goods:** $500 max per round (e.g., tables, chairs, reusable equipment).
*   **Venue Rental:** Generally capped at **$800**. Search for free/cheaper campus options first.
*   **Honoraria/Services:** $500 max per awardee per day (includes travel/lodging).

### ✅ What CAN be Funded
*   Food and non-alcoholic beverages (Plant-based strongly encouraged).
*   Publicity/Advertising for open events.
*   Supplies and equipment necessary for the event.
*   Honoraria for speakers (Lecture, panel, workshop).
*   Contracted services (Exercise classes, professional services).

### ❌ Specifically FORBIDDEN Uses
*   **Alcohol** (unless religious exemption is granted).
*   **Gifts/Prizes:** Gift cards, raffles, trophies, or any personal gifts.
*   **Bottled Water:** Not funded in keeping with sustainability goals.
*   **Non-compostable Cutlery:** Disposable plastic utensils are forbidden.
*   **Travel/Lodging:** For individuals (except as part of honoraria/PD/GSP awards).
*   **Apparel:** T-shirts or any custom club gear.
*   **Website Hosting:** Fees for creating or hosting sites.
*   **Select Events:** Board meetings, retreats, or events not open to all UCB grads.

### 📅 Deadlines & Receipts
*   **Submission:** See GA Calendar (usually mid-month for the following month).
*   **Receipts:** Must be submitted on CalLink within **30 days** of the event.
*   **Fall Deadline:** All funds must be spent and reclaimed by **Dec 31, 2026**.
*   **Spring Deadline:** All funds must be spent and reclaimed by **June 30, 2027**.
