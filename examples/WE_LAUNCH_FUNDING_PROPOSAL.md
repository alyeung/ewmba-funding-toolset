## 5. Strategic Rationale
The EWMBA Community Welcome Reception & Mixer is a vital student-run initiative led by the EWMBA Association to welcome and integrate peers into the UC Berkeley graduate community. Taking place from July 24-26, it aligns perfectly with the GA's Summer Large-Project Funding window (June 30 – Sept 11). By securing $1,500 in GA funding (of the $2,000 requested, due to round-specific caps), our student association can provide high-volume catering (Pizza/Cookies) and engagement supplies for up to 250 graduate students at an efficient cost of ~$6.00 per head. To ensure strict compliance with the GA's Location Restrictions, the GA-funded portion of this event will be hosted entirely on-campus at the Haas School of Business, with no GA funds used for any secondary off-campus activities (such as our team's planned visit to the Marriott Oakland). This peer-led event is essential for building inclusive community ties from day one and will be cross-promoted via the GA calendar to ensure full transparency and open access to the entire graduate student body. Securing these funds ensures the EWMBA Association can fulfill its mission of community building without depleting its limited student-run reserves.
