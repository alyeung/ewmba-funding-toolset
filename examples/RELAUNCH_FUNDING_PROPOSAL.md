## 2. Event Overview
*   **Purpose:** This student-led Community Kick-off Social & Networking Brunch is designed to facilitate cross-cohort mentorship and community integration for graduate students. The brunch serves as a central hub at Haas where students network and form groups for optional, self-guided exploration of the UC Berkeley ecosystem. These peer-led activities utilize free student access via the Cal 1 Card and include:
    *   **Lawrence Hall of Science:** Exploring "Science & Dinosaurs."
    *   **UC Botanical Gardens:** Walking through global nature slices.
    *   **BAMPFA:** Visiting the UC Berkeley Art Museum and Pacific Film Archive.
    *   **The Campanile:** Touring the bell tower for campus views.
    *   **Tilden Regional Park:** Self-organized hikes through regional trails.
    By providing a plant-based networking brunch as the foundation for these activities, the event fosters vibrant student life and long-term inclusive community ties.
*   **Date & Location:** Sunday, August 2, 2026, Haas School of Business Campus (Kick-off Social Location)
*   **Expected Attendance:** 
    *   Graduate Students: ~250 (New, continuing, and professional graduate students across UCB)
    *   Non-Graduate Students: ~10 (Faculty/Staff)

## 5. Strategic Rationale
The EWMBA Community DIY Networking Brunch is a high-impact student-run initiative focused on peer-to-peer mentorship. Held on Sunday, August 2, at the Haas School of Business, it falls within the GA's Summer Large-Project Funding window. By securing $2,000 in GA funding, we can provide high-volume, fully plant-based catering for 250 graduate students (~$8.00/head). This brunch facilitates a structured "Kick-off" where students build community ties before embarking on self-guided exploration of iconic campus resources (using free Cal 1 Card access). The GA-funded portion is hosted strictly on-campus to ensure compliance with location guidelines. By certifying as plant-based, we are leveraging the GA's secondary sustainability fund, ensuring we can deliver a high-quality community experience without depleting student-run reserves.
