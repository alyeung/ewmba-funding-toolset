# EWMBAA Officer Finance & CalLink Guide

This guide outlines the procedures and responsibilities for EWMBAA Executive Board members, ASUC/GA Agents, and Financial Requestors when submitting and approving expense reports in CalLink Finance.

Looking for the student-facing reimbursement checklist? See the **[4 Steps to Reimbursement Guidelines](./REIMBURSEMENT_GUIDELINES.md)**.

---

## 1. CalLink Roster Roles & Permissions

Only authorized officers on your organization's CalLink roster can manage finance operations:

* **Financial Requestor:**
  * Can create and submit Purchase Requests (PRs) on behalf of the organization.
  * Can view the organization's entire CalLink Finance portal.
  * **Cannot** internally approve or move requests from Stage 1 to Stage 2.
  * *Assignment:* Can be assigned by any Signatory or Agent directly on the CalLink roster.

* **ASUC/GA Agent ("Agent"):**
  * Holds primary financial responsibility for the organization.
  * Can submit PRs AND internally "approve" Stage 1 PRs by advancing them to Stage 2.
  * *Important Rule:* **An Agent cannot approve a request where they are the payee.** Another Agent on the roster must review and approve it.
  * *Assignment:* Requires completing the 6-module ASUC Finance bCourses training and passing all quizzes with $\ge 80\%$ average.

## 2. Submitting a Purchase Request (PR) for a Student

When a student submits their reimbursement table and consolidated PDF to `ewmbaa-haas@`, a Financial Requestor or Agent submits the PR on CalLink:

1. **Log into CalLink:** Go to [callink.berkeley.edu](https://callink.berkeley.edu/).
2. **Navigate to Finance:** Click your profile icon $\rightarrow$ **Manage** $\rightarrow$ Select your organization $\rightarrow$ **Finance**.
3. **Create Request:** Click **Create Request** $\rightarrow$ **Create Purchase Request**.
4. **Select Category & Account:**
   * Category: Select **Reimbursement**.
   * From Account: Choose the appropriate account (e.g., Graduate Assembly allocation or EWMBAA Miscellaneous/Club reserves). *Never select the SUMMARY account.*
5. **Enter Payee Information:**
   * Enter the **student's** first name, last name, physical mailing address, phone, and email from their submission table.
   * Enter the student's **Unique ID (UID)** from the [Berkeley Directory](https://www.berkeley.edu/directory/) or their CalCentral profile.
   * *Critical:* All payee fields must match the student's details so the payment or direct deposit issues to them, not to you.
6. **Attach Documentation:**
   * Upload the student's **consolidated PDF** (containing both the itemized merchant receipt and credit card/bank statement screenshot).
7. **Submit:** Click **Submit Request**. The PR will enter **Stage 1 (Unapproved)**.

## 3. Reviewing Stage 1 & Advancing to Stage 2 (Agent Review)

Once a PR is in Stage 1, an **ASUC/GA Agent** (who is not the payee) must review and advance it:

1. **Open the Request:** On your organization's Finance page, click the Stage 1 PR.
2. **Review the Documentation Box (Top Left):**
   * Confirm the receipt is fully itemized and reflects a zero remaining balance.
   * Confirm proof of payment shows the payee's name, last 4 card digits, date, and vendor.
   * Confirm payee name, mailing address, and UID are accurate.
3. **Leave Correction Comments (If Needed):**
   * If any corrections are needed (wrong category, incorrect account, missing attachment), post a comment in the **Discussion box** at the bottom of the page *before* moving the stage.
   * ASUC Finance staff will review discussion comments and apply corrections during Stage 2.
4. **Advance the Stage:**
   * Locate the **Accounting Box** (top right).
   * Click the **Stage** dropdown and select **Stage 2**.
   * In the **Stage Change Comment** box, leave a permanent note (e.g., *"Approved - looks good to go!"* or *"Approved with corrections in discussion"*).
   * Click **Save** in the top right corner of the Accounting Box.
5. **Routing:** The request will log *"Moved to Stage 2"* in the history and route to ASUC Finance staff for payment processing.

## 4. Splitting Expenses Across Accounts or POs

If an expense exceeds the balance of a single account or needs to be divided across funding sources:

* **Method 1: Two Separate Purchase Requests (Recommended for separate POs)**
  * Submit two separate PRs referencing the same invoice/receipt.
  * Set the **Requested Amount** in each PR to reflect the portion paid from each account.
  * Select the respective finance account on each PR.

* **Method 2: Single PR with Split Instructions**
  * Submit one PR for the full invoice/receipt amount under the primary account.
  * In the **Request Description** and **Question 5 (Special Instructions)**, explicitly list the second account code and the exact dollar amount to pull from each account.
  * ASUC Finance staff will split the ledger during Stage 2 review.

## 5. Graduate Assembly (GA) Funding Compliance Rules

When reimbursing expenses funded by the Graduate Assembly:

* **Submission Deadlines:** PRs must be submitted on CalLink within **30 days** of the event. All GA funds must be claimed by **December 31** (Fall) or **June 30** (Spring); funds do not roll over.
* **Per-Attendee Food Limits:**
  * Maximum **$20.00** per UC Berkeley graduate/professional student attendee.
  * Maximum **$1.00** per other attendee (undergraduates, staff, non-students).
* **Banned Items:** The GA will **never** reimburse:
  * Alcohol or bar tabs.
  * Single-use bottled water or non-compostable disposable utensils.
  * Organization-branded merchandise, apparel, or promotional items.
  * Speaker gifts or honoraria for UC Berkeley faculty/staff.
* **Travel & Tips:**
  * Personal vehicle travel is reimbursed on a standard mileage basis (requires a Google Maps route map; odometer photos not accepted).
  * Food and service tips are capped at **20%**. Any tip exceeding 20% requires written justification.
* **Contracted Services:**
  * Services performed by individuals (e.g., photographers, DJs, videographers, caterers) cannot be reimbursed out-of-pocket.
  * They must be paid directly by ASUC using a signed **Payment Voucher** and completed tax forms (W-9 or W-8BEN).
* *Full Policy Reference:* See the complete [2026-27 GA Funding Guide](./templates/2026-27%20Funding%20Guide.md).

## 6. EWMBAA Internal Club Funding Rules

For expenses funded through EWMBAA internal/club reserves:

* **Pre-Approval:** All purchases must be pre-approved by the EWMBAA Executive Board / VP of Finance before purchase.
* **GA-Ineligible Expenses:** Items that cannot be paid via GA funding (e.g., alcohol, alumni/selective mixers, thank-you gifts) may be budgeted under EWMBAA internal reserves, subject to board approval.
* **Payment Routing:** Internal club expenditures are still routed through CalLink Finance using the organization's Miscellaneous/Club reserve account.
