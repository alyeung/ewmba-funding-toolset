# EWMBAA Reimbursement Guidelines

A 3-step checklist for authorized out-of-pocket event expenses.

---

### 1. Set Up Direct Deposit (Once per year)

Sign up via the ASUC DocuSign portal to receive electronic payments:

👉 **[ASUC Direct Deposit Authorization Form](https://na3.docusign.net/Member/PowerFormSigning.aspx?PowerFormId=30413e7e-b85b-4c0b-8f05-6ffe10bafb77&env=na3&acct=620e8a46-493a-4013-a8aa-1bce714f8b8a&v=2)**

* Attach a voided check or bank statement verifying your name and account number.
* *Note: ASUC Finance is independent and does not link to UCPath / CalCentral direct deposit records.*

---

### 2. Save Receipts to 1 PDF

Place two items into a document and export/print as a **single PDF**:

1. **Itemized Merchant Receipt** — Must show vendor name, date, items, total, and zero remaining balance.
2. **Proof of Payment** — Credit card or bank statement screenshot showing your name, last 4 card digits, date, amount, and vendor.

*Restrictions: Cash, Venmo, Zelle, and PayPal transactions cannot be reimbursed.*

---

### 3. Email the Finance Team

Copy the template below, paste it into an email, attach your **PDF**, and send:

* **To:** `ewmbaa-haas@`
* **Cc:** Allan (`allan_yeung@`), Jason (`jalau@`), Nick (`nick_perez@`)

```text
Subject: Reimbursement Request - [Event Name] - [Your Name]

First Name: 
Last Name: 
Mailing Address: 
Unique ID (UID): 
Phone Number: 
Email Address: 

[Attach: Consolidated Receipt & Statement PDF]
```

*(Find your UID in the [Berkeley Directory](https://www.berkeley.edu/directory/) or via CalCentral Profile $\rightarrow$ User ID).*
