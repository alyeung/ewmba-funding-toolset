# UC Berkeley GA & EWMBAA Reimbursement Guidelines

This guide details the requirements and procedures for submitting expense reports and receiving reimbursements through the **Graduate Assembly (GA)** and the **Evening & Weekend MBA Association (EWMBAA)**.

---

## 4 Steps to Reimbursement (Send this to Payees)

If you made an authorized out-of-pocket purchase for an event, follow these 4 steps to submit your reimbursement request. 
*(Note: If you want reimbursements to go to different people, **each person** must submit their own request and provide the information below. Reimbursements can only be issued to the person who actually made the purchase.)*

### Step 1: Set Up Direct Deposit (DocuSign)
To receive payment via direct deposit (EFT) rather than a paper check in the mail, you must complete the ASUC-specific authorization. 
*(Note: ASUC Finance is not connected to the UC Berkeley payment system and does not have access to CalCentral or UCPath direct deposit records. Payees MUST sign up for ASUC direct deposit using the link below even if they already have university direct deposit set up.)*
1. Complete the secure [ASUC/GA Direct Deposit Authorization Form](https://na3.docusign.net/Member/PowerFormSigning.aspx?PowerFormId=30413e7e-b85b-4c0b-8f05-6ffe10bafb77&env=na3&acct=620e8a46-493a-4013-a8aa-1bce714f8b8a&v=2).
2. Upload a **voided check or a current bank statement** showing your name and account number to the DocuSign. Do not upload other files.

### Step 2: Look Up Your Payee Details & Unique ID (UID)
Ensure you have the following information ready:
1. **Payee Mailing Address:** First Name, Last Name, Street Address, City, State, and Zip Code (must match the name on the bank account).
2. **Unique ID (UID):** Look up your UID in the [Berkeley People Directory](https://www.berkeley.edu/directory/) by searching your name.
   * *Backup Method:* If your listing is suppressed or not found in the directory, log into [CalCentral](https://calcentral.berkeley.edu) -> Upper Right Profile icon -> **Personal Profile** -> **User ID**.
3. **Contact Details:** Your phone number and email address (for payment status updates).

### Step 3: Prepare Receipts & Proof of Payment (Consolidated PDF)
All reimbursements require original itemized receipts and proof of payment consolidated into a single PDF:
1. **Original Itemized Receipt:** Must show vendor name, date, items, total, and zero remaining balance. (Non-itemized credit card receipts will be rejected).
2. **Proof of Payment (Billing Statement):** A screenshot or billing statement from your credit card or checking account showing your name, last 4 digits of the card/account, transaction date, amount, and the vendor.
3. **Consolidate into One PDF:** Place both the merchant receipt and the billing statement into a Word document, then export/print as a **PDF** to share.
*Warning: Cash/check payments from personal accounts and peer-to-peer payments (Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, Cashapp) are strictly banned and cannot be reimbursed.*

## CalLink Instructions for Officers / Financial Requestors

Only designated Financial Requestors or Agents can submit purchase requests. When submitting a reimbursement request for a student:
1. Log into [CalLink](https://callink.berkeley.edu/).
2. Navigate to your organization's page: Click your profile icon -> **Manage** -> Select your organization -> **Finance**.
3. Click **Create Request** -> **Create Purchase Request**.
4. Set the category to **Reimbursement** and select the correct account (GA Funding or internal Miscellaneous reserves).
5. In the **Payee Information** section, enter the student's payee details (first/last name, mailing address, email, phone) and their Unique ID (UID).
   *(Note: The payee name and details must be the student's, not yours, to ensure direct deposit is issued to the correct bank account.)*
6. Attach the student's itemized receipt and bank statement screenshot.
7. Submit the request. The VP of Finance/Agent will review the request (Stage 1) and move it to Stage 2.

## Graduate Assembly (GA) Compliance Rules

When using GA funding (as opposed to EWMBAA internal reserves), the following rules apply:

*   **Timeline:** Submit the PR on CalLink within **30 days** of the event. All GA funds must be claimed by **Dec 31** (Fall) or **June 30** (Spring); they do not roll over.
*   **Food Limits:** Capped at **$20.00** per UCB graduate student and **$1.00** per other attendee (undergrads, staff, speakers).
*   **Banned Items:** Alcohol, bottled water, non-compostable disposable cutlery, logo apparel/custom gear, and speaker thank-you gifts cannot be paid using GA funds. 
*   **Travel & Tips:** Personal car travel is reimbursed on a mileage basis (requires a route map, odometer photos not accepted). Service tips are capped at 20% (over 20% requires written justification).
*   **Contracted Services:** Services by individuals (e.g. photographers, tutors) cannot be reimbursed out-of-pocket. They must be paid directly by ASUC using a signed **Payment Voucher** and tax forms (W-9/W-8BEN).

## EWMBAA Internal Funding Rules

For expenses being covered by the EWMBAA's budget:

*   **Pre-Approval:** All purchases must be pre-approved by the EWMBAA Executive Board / Finance Committee.
*   **Use of Reserves:** Expenses that violate GA compliance (e.g., speaker thank-you gifts, selective mixers, alcohol) must be budgeted and approved under EWMBAA internal/club reserves.
